Teacher Rules & Regulations

  • Use your own account and keep your password private.
  • Mark attendance accurately and on the correct date.
  • Submit student reports honestly and before deadlines.
  • Protect student information; do not share private records outside the school.
  • Use school chat, files and discussions for respectful academic communication.
  • Do not attempt to access or change records outside your assigned permissions.
  • Report incorrect records or security concerns to the Head Teacher or Admin.

Privacy: School records are stored in the connected Firebase project only after successful database operations. Access depends on Firebase Security Rules configured by the school administrator.
